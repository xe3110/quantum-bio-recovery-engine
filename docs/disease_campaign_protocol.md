# Disease campaign protocol — adding a disease, and what the engine does with it

This is the standing procedure. Two diseases are registered — multiple
sclerosis and Parkinson's — and the point of writing this down is that the
third, fourth, and fifth should not require reading any scoring code.

A **campaign** is everything the engine does for one disease: rank the agents
that already exist ([combination screen](#3-the-combination-screen)), and
specify one that does not ([de novo design](denovo_design_protocol.md)). Both
read the same registry entry. Neither names a disease anywhere in its logic.

| Disease | Registry entry | Combination screen | Design campaign |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multiple sclerosis | `data/diseases/multiple_sclerosis.json` | [protocol](ms_publication_protocol.md) — pairs only, `ms_scoring` | [protocol](denovo_design_protocol.md) |
| Parkinson's disease | `data/diseases/parkinsons.json` | [protocol](parkinsons_screen.md) — k = 1, 2, 3, `combination_scoring` | [protocol](denovo_design_protocol.md) |

Everything below is **discovery-stage hypothesis generation**. No output of any
campaign is a clinical recommendation, and every campaign inherits every
limitation of its inputs.

---

## 1. What a disease is, to this engine

A registry entry under `data/diseases/<identifier>.json`, loaded as a
`DiseaseContext` ([core/models/disease.py](../core/models/disease.py)). It
carries five things, and the engine reads all of them from there rather than
from any constant in any module:

| Field | What it is | Why it belongs to the disease, not the code |
|---|---|---|
| `vocabulary.pathways` | The biological processes the signature is annotated against | A pathway list is a claim about what the disease *is* |
| `vocabulary.therapeutic_axes` | The distinct ways a therapy can help | MS and Parkinson's share **zero** axes; a shared constant would be a lie |
| `vocabulary.risk_domains` + `risk_weights` | What constrains a regimen, and how strongly | MS therapy is constrained by infection and malignancy; Parkinson's by dyskinesia, impulse-control disorders, and psychosis. Weighted by how strongly each domain ends a regimen in *that* population |
| `delivery` | Whether a molecule must reach a sanctuary compartment | For Parkinson's the pathology is intraparenchymal from the outset, so CNS exposure is a gate. `sanctuary_rationale` records why, because a constraint without a stated reason gets relaxed by whoever next finds it inconvenient |
| `gene_aliases` | Symbol drift between the interactome and HGNC | STRING v12 still calls glucocerebrosidase `GBA`; HGNC calls it `GBA1`. Unmapped, the most common genetic risk factor in PD silently has zero network leverage |
| `data` | Paths to signature, network, panel, druggability, **structures** | Loading fails loudly if any of the first four is missing. `structures` is optional in the loader and that is a trap — see §2 |

`load_disease` validates that every declared data file exists and that
`risk_weights` names no domain absent from `risk_domains`, so a typo in a
registry entry fails at load rather than as a strange score later.

## 2. The four inputs, and what each has to satisfy

**Signature** — `data/<id>_expression.csv`. Columns `gene`, `logFC`,
`desired_direction` (−1 or +1), `pathway`, `confidence`. Gene weight is
`|logFC| × confidence`.

> The `desired_direction` column is the one methodological choice worth
> defending on its own. Raw log fold-change is the **wrong** optimisation
> target, because compensatory transcripts rise as part of a protective
> response. `HMOX1` rises in MS as an antioxidant response; a naive "reverse
> the disease signature" objective therefore scores dimethyl fumarate — an
> approved therapy — as harmful. Scoring targets the curated *therapeutic*
> direction instead. **This annotation is a curation judgement and is the first
> thing a reviewer should challenge.**

**Panel** — `data/drugs/<id>_panel_v*.json`, generated by a curation tool under
`tools/` that **fails the build** if any target gene is absent from the
signature, so a typo cannot enter the panel silently. Per record: mechanism
class, `target_family`, evidence tier, route, `cns_penetration`, `compartment`,
`therapeutic_axes`, `half_life_class`, `safety_burden` over the disease's own
risk domains, `target_uncertainty`, and signed `target_effects`.

> `target_effects` are **signed, unitless directional hypotheses in [−1, 1]** —
> not affinities, exposures, doses, or clinical-response values. Absolute
> scores therefore have no units and only relative comparisons are meaningful.

**Interactome** — `data/networks/string_<id>_network.tsv`. STRING v12,
*H. sapiens*, confidence ≥ 0.4, committed so results are reproducible offline
and the exact network is auditable.

**Druggability** — `data/targets/druggability_<id>.json`, per-target
small-molecule tractability and target class. Used by the design campaign to
keep a designed arm off a target no small molecule can reach (α-synuclein in
PD, CD20 in MS).

**Known structures** — `data/chemistry/<id>_known_structures.json`, SMILES plus
literature formula and mass for the small-molecule members of the panel. This is
the reference set the design campaign measures novelty against.

> **Declare this, even though the loader treats it as optional.** Novelty is
> assessed against `known_structures() + library.parent_structures()`, and
> `known_structures()` returns an empty list when the registry omits the path.
> A campaign run without it compares its designs only against the fragments they
> were assembled from, reports a plausible number, and gives no indication that
> the comparison was reduced. Parkinson's ran its first design campaign this way
> and reported a design as more novel than it is — see
> [the de novo protocol](denovo_design_protocol.md#the-novelty-figure-this-design-used-to-carry-was-wrong-2026-08-28).
> Two tests now fail if a registered disease omits the set or fills it with
> chemical matter the fragment library already had.

### Controls, declared in the panel before any ranking

| Control | What it tests | Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| `positive_redundancy` | Two agents that duplicate each other | **Must** be excluded from the primary ranking |
| `safety_penalty` | Withdrawn or heavily restricted agents | Must not reach the top of the ranking |
| `negative_efficacy` | Agents whose trials in this disease failed | See below |

Negative-efficacy controls test a **known limitation**, not a pass/fail
criterion. The screen scores mechanism and transcriptional direction, not trial
outcome, so a mechanistically coherent agent that failed in phase 3 can still
rank well. Such appearances are **reported, never filtered**, and they bound
what the method can claim.

## 3. The combination screen

[core/biology/combination_scoring.py](../core/biology/combination_scoring.py)
scores a combination of **any order** — k = 1 (monotherapy), 2, 3, … — against
a `DiseaseContext`. Monotherapy runs through the same function as a triple.
That is deliberate: if singles and combinations were scored by different code,
any difference between them would be partly an artefact of the two
implementations.

Every k-ary term is defined as the mean of its pairwise form, so **k = 2
reduces exactly to the pairwise definitions** and k ≥ 3 has a definition that
privileges no member.

### Parameter blocks

**Efficacy** (defined identically at every order) — `signed_reversal`,
`reversal_efficiency`, `gene_coverage`, `pathway_coverage`, `axis_coverage`,
`counter_therapeutic`.

**Efficacy comparison** — `reversal_gain_over_best_single`,
`score_gain_over_best_subset`, `additivity_ratio`.

**Pharmacology / delivery** — `target_complementarity`,
`compartment_complementarity`, `cns_reach`, `regimen_burden`.

**Network topology** — `network_separation`, `proximity_z_min/max`,
`complementary_exposure`.

**Evidence and risk** — `evidence_score`, `uncertainty`, `safety_union`,
`safety_overlap`, `worst_risk_domain`.

### Three modelling choices worth stating

1. **Bliss independence, not clipping.** Same-direction effects on one gene
   combine as `a + b − ab`, saturating at 1 without the discontinuity hard
   clipping introduces. Opposing effects add, so antagonism is represented
   rather than hidden.
2. **Reversal is normalised twice.** Absolute reversal over a ~100-gene
   signature spans a narrow range because any one agent engages a handful of
   genes, which would let low-efficacy/low-risk agents win the composite on
   bonus terms alone. `reversal_efficiency` (therapeutic movement ÷ engaged
   signal) restores a comparable [0, 1] scale; coverage is scored separately
   against a declared `broad_coverage_reference`.
3. **Compartment scoring follows the registry.** Where
   `requires_cns_exposure` is set the term is CNS *coverage*, because a regimen
   that never reaches the parenchyma cannot modify an intraparenchymal disease.
   Where it is not, the term is compartment *spread*, which is what rewards the
   peripheral/central split that matters in MS.

### Redundancy and the order ladder

A combination is excluded from the primary ranking if **any pair inside it**
shares a `mechanism_class`, shares a `safety_class`, shares a `target_family`,
or exceeds `max_target_overlap`. Excluded rows are still computed and written
to the full CSV, so the exclusion is auditable rather than invisible.

Order k + 1 is enumerated only over combinations all of whose k-faces survived,
so a triple can never be built around a duplicate pair. This is what keeps the
higher-order enumeration both honest and affordable.

### Comparing across orders — the rule

> **`priority_score` is comparable only within a fixed order.** It includes
> terms a single agent cannot earn by construction: target complementarity,
> compartment complementarity, network separation. Ranking a monotherapy
> against a pair by composite score would conclude that combinations win, when
> what they actually did was collect bonuses that are zero at k = 1.

Across orders, compare the **efficacy block**, which is defined identically at
every k. Three fields carry the comparison:

- `reversal_gain_over_best_single` — what the combination adds over its own
  best member.
- `score_gain_over_best_subset` — for k ≥ 3, what the k-th agent adds over the
  best (k−1)-subset it contains. **Negative means the third agent does not earn
  its place.**
- `additivity_ratio` — combined reversal ÷ sum of the members' solo reversals.
  Below 1 the members are covering the same signal. The median is 1.0 wherever
  most members have disjoint targets, so read the *tail*: `fraction_subadditive`
  is the informative half.

A test asserts that `priority_score` never appears in the comparable set.

## 4. Statistical treatment

[core/biology/combination_statistics.py](../core/biology/combination_statistics.py).
A ranked list is not a result on its own.

- **Permutation null** — each agent's effect *magnitudes* are reassigned to
  random signature genes, preserving target-set size and the magnitude
  multiset, so the null isolates *which* genes an agent hits from *how hard*
  and *how many*. Add-one corrected, so *p* is never 0. Topology is excluded
  from the null on both sides, since including a fixed term would inflate both
  equally and tell a reader nothing.
- **BH-FDR** across the scored set at each order → `q_value`.
- **Pareto front** over nine objectives, computed without weights: which
  combinations survive *any* weighting.
- **Weight sensitivity** — every weight jittered ±50%, reported as Spearman
  correlation and top-K overlap against the declared weights.
- **Bootstrap stability** — target effects resampled under each record's own
  `target_uncertainty`, reported as score and **rank** 95% CIs plus a top-K
  Jaccard index.
- **Mechanism-stratum enrichment** — combinations aggregated by therapeutic-axis
  stratum, Mann-Whitney against all other strata, BH-corrected, with bootstrap
  CIs on each stratum median.

### The finding that keeps recurring

In both campaigns run so far, **individual combination ranks are not stable**
under the curated target-effect uncertainty (top-K Jaccard ≈ 0.2), while
**stratum medians are**. The runner prints an explicit warning whenever that
Jaccard falls below 0.5.

> **The unit of inference is the mechanism stratum, not the named combination.**
> A leaderboard of specific combinations is not a defensible result from this
> class of screen. Named combinations should be read as *illustrative of their
> stratum*.

This is also why `--max-per-drug` exists: without it, one broadly-acting agent
occupies most of the leaderboard and hides the mechanistic diversity the screen
exists to surface.

## 5. Provenance

Every result file carries a `run_provenance` block
([core/provenance.py](../core/provenance.py)): a **content digest of every
input file**, the git revision **and whether the tree was dirty**, the Python
and package versions, the chemistry backend, the seed, and the exact command.

A version string in a metadata block does not survive someone editing a CSV; a
digest does. A clean revision hash on a modified tree is worse than no hash at
all. Results under `experiments/*/results/` are **disposable run snapshots**;
everything under `data/` is curated, reviewed, and the thing a reviewer is
expected to argue with.

## 6. Adding the next disease — checklist

No scoring code should need editing. If it does, that is the bug.

1. **Signature** → `data/<id>_expression.csv`. Annotate every gene
   `pathogenic` / `protective_deficit` / `compensatory` and set
   `desired_direction` from the *therapeutic* direction, not the observed one.
2. **Interactome** → `python -m tools.fetch_string_network` for the seed gene
   set; commit the `.tsv` and its `.meta.json`.
3. **Druggability** → `data/targets/druggability_<id>.json`. Anything below
   ~0.2 small-molecule tractability is a transcriptional readout, not a target
   an arm may be pointed at.
4. **Panel** → `tools/curate_<id>_panel.py`, following
   [tools/curate_pd_panel.py](../tools/curate_pd_panel.py). It must fail on any
   target gene absent from the signature. Declare the three control sets.
5. **Known structures** → `data/chemistry/<id>_known_structures.json`, one entry
   per small-molecule panel member with SMILES, literature formula, and mass.
   State the omissions and why (biologics have no structure; a development
   compound with no settled public structure should be omitted rather than
   guessed). `tests/test_chemistry.py` re-derives formula and mass from every
   SMILES and fails on disagreement, so a mistyped structure cannot enter
   silently.
6. **Registry entry** → `data/diseases/<id>.json` with the vocabularies,
   `risk_weights` and their rationale, the delivery constraint and its
   rationale, any `gene_aliases`, the `data.structures` path from step 5, and a
   `provenance` block that is honest about how the signature was derived.
7. **Runner** → copy
   [experiments/parkinsons/run_combination_screen.py](../experiments/parkinsons/run_combination_screen.py)
   and change the `DISEASE` constant. Nothing else in it is disease-specific.
8. **Tests** → add the identifier to `DISEASES` in
   [tests/test_multi_disease.py](../tests/test_multi_disease.py) and
   [tests/test_combination_scoring.py](../tests/test_combination_scoring.py).
   Both are parametrised, so the new disease is held to every existing claim
   without new test code.
9. **Doc** → a screen protocol beside this one, stating the disease's own
   caveats. The generic ones are here; do not restate them, state what is
   *different*.

### Two checks the test suite already enforces

- The design layer must not import disease-specific scoring. A layer that says
  it is disease-agnostic while importing `ms_scoring` is not, whatever its
  registry contains.
- Registered diseases must have genuinely different vocabularies. MS and PD
  share **no** therapeutic axis and only generic organ toxicity (`cardiac`,
  `hepatic`) among risk domains. Two diseases with the same vocabulary would
  not test the abstraction at all.
- Every registered disease must declare a novelty reference set that overlaps
  its own panel and adds chemical matter beyond the fragment library. An
  optional field that silently degrades a result is worse than a required one.

## 7. Standing limitations of every campaign

These apply to every disease and do not need restating in a per-disease doc.

- Signatures are curated, not derived from a cohort. Replace with a versioned,
  batch-corrected, phenotype-stratified human dataset before publication, and
  report cohort, tissue, pipeline, genome build, normalisation, and the
  differential-expression model.
- `desired_direction` is a curation judgement (§2).
- `target_effects` are directional hypotheses; only relative comparisons mean
  anything.
- Panel targets are curated *from* the signature gene set, so disease-module
  proximity is partly circular. The **separation** term carries the independent
  topological signal; the proximity *z* arm is descriptive.
- Individual combination ranks are unstable (§4).
- Transcript-level direction is a weak proxy for clinical benefit.
- **No pharmacokinetic or drug–drug-interaction model exists.** Co-administration
  cost is represented only by route burden and half-life spread in
  `regimen_burden`. This is the largest open gap, it binds hardest on diseases
  treated with chronic oral polypharmacy, and it is the next model to build.
- **Bounded by the panel.** A screen can only return a molecule someone has
  already made. Lifting that bound is what the
  [de novo design campaign](denovo_design_protocol.md) is for.
- **Modality-blind.** Antibodies and small molecules are ranked side by side.
  The design campaign corrects this with per-target druggability.
