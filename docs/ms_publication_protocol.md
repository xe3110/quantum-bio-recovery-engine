# Multiple-sclerosis combination-candidate screen — protocol

A deterministic, multi-parameter, discovery-stage screen over a curated panel of
**74 MS candidates** (2,701 pairs; 2,016 scored at the default `phase_2`
evidence floor) against a **112-gene directional disease signature** and a
**cached STRING interactome**.

It is **not** a clinical combination recommender. Concurrent immunomodulation
carries additive infection and malignancy risk that no in-silico score can
quantify. Every output is a hypothesis for experimental validation.

This screen ranks agents that **already exist**. The companion
[de novo design protocol](denovo_design_protocol.md) covers the campaign that
specifies and assembles one that does not, over the same disease model,
signature, and interactome.

## 2. Inputs

- **`data/ms_expression_v3.csv`** — 112 genes with `logFC`, `pathway`,
  curation `confidence`, and an explicit **`desired_direction`**.

  The direction column is a deliberate methodological choice. Raw log
  fold-change is the wrong optimisation target for **compensatory** transcripts:
  `HMOX1`, `SOD2`, `TREM2`, and `PDGFRA` rise in MS as part of a protective
  response, so a naive "reverse the disease signature" objective would penalise
  Nrf2 activators — i.e. it would score dimethyl fumarate, an approved therapy,
  as harmful. Genes are therefore annotated `pathogenic` (73), `protective_deficit`
  (35), or `compensatory` (4), and scoring targets the curated therapeutic
  direction. **This annotation is a curation judgement and is the single
  assumption a reviewer should challenge first.**

  This signature is *illustrative*. Before publication, replace it with a
  versioned, batch-corrected, phenotype-stratified human dataset and report
  cohort, tissue/cell type, pipeline, genome build, normalisation, and the
  differential-expression model.

- **`data/drugs/ms_panel_v3.json`** — 74 records, regenerated by
  `python -m tools.curate_ms_panel_v3`, which validates every record and fails
  if any target gene is absent from the signature. Per record: mechanism class,
  evidence tier, route, `cns_penetration`, `compartment`, `therapeutic_axes`,
  `half_life_class`, a 7-domain `safety_burden`, `target_uncertainty`, and signed
  `target_effects`.

  `target_effects` are **signed, unitless directional hypotheses in [-1, 1]** —
  not affinities, exposures, doses, or clinical-response values.

- **`data/networks/string_ms_network.tsv`** — STRING v12, *H. sapiens*,
  confidence ≥ 0.4, 150-node first-shell expansion: 261 nodes, 14,784 edges.
  Committed so results are reproducible offline and the exact interactome is
  auditable. Refresh with `python -m tools.fetch_string_network`.
  `GPX1` is absent from the network and is excluded from topology metrics only.

## 3. Scoring parameters

**Efficacy** — `signed_reversal`, `reversal_efficiency`, `gene_coverage`,
`reversal_gain_over_best_single`, `counter_therapeutic`, `pathway_coverage`,
`axis_coverage`, `target_complementarity`.

**Pharmacology** — `compartment_complementarity`, `cns_reach`,
`regimen_compatibility`.

**Network topology** — `network_separation`, `proximity_z_a`, `proximity_z_b`,
`complementary_exposure`.

**Evidence and risk** — `evidence_score`, `uncertainty`, `safety_union`,
`safety_overlap`, `worst_risk_domain`.

Three modelling choices are worth stating explicitly:

1. **Bliss independence, not clipping.** Same-direction effects on one gene
   combine as `a + b − ab`, saturating at 1 without the discontinuity hard
   clipping introduces; opposing effects add, so antagonism is represented.
2. **Reversal is normalised twice.** Absolute reversal over a 112-gene
   signature spans only [0, 0.1] because any one agent engages a handful of
   genes, which would let low-efficacy/low-risk agents win the composite on the
   bonus terms alone. `reversal_efficiency` (therapeutic movement ÷ engaged
   signal) restores a comparable [0, 1] scale; coverage is scored separately
   against a declared `BROAD_COVERAGE_REFERENCE = 0.25`.
3. **Compartment complementarity is scored deliberately.** Compartmentalised
   inflammation behind an intact blood-brain barrier is a leading explanation for
   why peripheral immunosuppression plateaus in progressive MS, so covering both
   the peripheral and CNS compartments earns credit.

Pairs sharing a `mechanism_class` or any `safety_class` are computed and
retained in the full CSV but **excluded from the primary ranking**.

## 4. Statistical treatment

- **Permutation null** (400 draws/pair): each drug's effect *magnitudes* are
  reassigned to random signature genes, preserving target-set size and the
  magnitude multiset. This isolates *which* genes an agent hits from *how hard*
  and *how many*. Add-one corrected, so *p* is never 0.
- **BH-FDR** across all scored pairs → `q_value`.
- **Pareto front** over 7 objectives, computed without weights: which pairs
  survive *any* weighting.
- **Weight sensitivity**: every weight jittered ±50%, 40 trials, reported as
  Spearman correlation against the declared weights.
- **Bootstrap stability**: 200 draws resampling target effects under each
  record's stated `target_uncertainty`, reported as score and **rank** 95% CIs
  and a top-K Jaccard index.
- **Mechanism-stratum enrichment**: pairs aggregated by unordered therapeutic-axis
  pair, Mann-Whitney vs all other strata, BH-corrected, with bootstrap CIs on
  each stratum median.

## 5. Controls, declared before ranking

| Control | Members | Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| Positive redundancy | Natalizumab + its biosimilar | must be excluded (same mechanism) |
| Safety penalty | Daclizumab (withdrawn), Cyclophosphamide, Mitoxantrone | must not reach the top of the ranking |
| Negative efficacy | Ustekinumab, High-dose biotin, Opicinumab, Evobrutinib | failed MS trials; see caveat below |

Negative-efficacy controls test a **known limitation** rather than a pass/fail
criterion. The screen scores mechanism and transcriptional direction, not trial
outcome, so an agent that is mechanistically coherent but clinically
unsuccessful can still rank well. Such appearances are **reported, never
filtered**, and they bound what the method can claim.

## 6. Reading the results — the important part

**The unit of inference is the mechanism stratum, not the named pair.**

The bootstrap shows individual pair ranks are unstable under the curated
target-effect uncertainty: top-30 Jaccard ≈ 0.25, with rank 95% CIs spanning
hundreds of positions. A leaderboard of specific pairs is therefore **not** a
defensible result from this screen, and the runner prints an explicit warning
whenever that Jaccard falls below 0.5.

Stratum medians, by contrast, carry bootstrap intervals that are narrow
relative to the spread between strata. The robust finding is the ordering of
mechanism combinations — with `cns_innate + remyelination` at the top and
`immunomodulation + immunomodulation` at the bottom. Named pairs should be read
as *illustrative of their stratum*.

This is also why `--max-per-drug` exists: without it a single broadly-acting
agent occupies most of the leaderboard and hides the mechanistic diversity the
screen exists to surface.

## 7. Reproduce

```bash
python -m tools.fetch_string_network          # optional; cached copy is committed
python -m tools.curate_ms_panel_v3            # regenerate + validate the panel
python -m experiments.ms.run_extended_screen --top 30 --seed 7
python -m experiments.ms.plot_screen
pytest -q tests/
```

`--quick` runs a reduced-draw smoke test. Outputs carry a provenance block
recording git revision, Python/NumPy versions, platform, seed, and the exact
command. Every run is deterministic under a stated seed.

## 8. Required validation before publication

1. Pre-register subtype, outcome, inclusion/exclusion, weights, and the
   validation split before looking at rankings.
2. Replace the illustrative signature with one discovery and one independent
   validation cohort; analyse RRMS, active SPMS, and PPMS separately.
3. Map targets to stable HGNC/Ensembl identifiers; record database version,
   assay type, potency, direction, and evidence source per edge. Re-derive
   `target_effects` from measured pharmacology rather than curation.
4. Reduce `target_uncertainty` with real affinity data — the rank instability in
   §6 is driven by it, and it is the highest-value fix available.
5. Test dose matrices in disease-relevant human systems (immune-cell and
   glia/oligodendrocyte co-cultures). Report raw response, dose-response fits,
   and Bliss, HSA, Loewe, and ZIP — not a model score alone.
6. Validate top strata in an appropriate MS model; assess exposure, BBB
   penetration, PK, immunogenicity, infection risk, and PD biomarkers.
7. Release code, frozen inputs, seed, full rankings including excluded pairs,
   and the control analysis.

## 9. Known limitations

- The expression signature is illustrative, not a real cohort.
- `desired_direction` is a curation judgement (§2).
- `target_effects` are directional hypotheses, so absolute scores have no units
  and only relative comparisons are meaningful.
- Panel targets were curated *from* the signature gene set, so disease-module
  proximity is partly circular; the **separation** term carries the independent
  topological signal, and the proximity *z* arm should be treated as descriptive.
- Pair ranks are unstable (§6).
- Transcript-level direction is a weak proxy for clinical benefit (§5).
- Pairwise only — no higher-order combinations, and no pharmacokinetic
  interaction modelling.
- **Bounded by the panel.** The screen can only ever return a molecule someone
  has already made. Its own headline finding is that peripheral-only
  combinations rank last while the remyelination axis has no approved agent at
  all — a gap the screen can identify and cannot, in principle, fill. See §10.
- **Modality-blind.** Antibodies and small molecules are ranked side by side
  with no representation of what each modality can reach. A top-ranked pair may
  combine an intravenous antibody with an oral agent and say nothing about
  whether either reaches the CNS compartment where the disease persists.

## 10. Where this screen ends and design begins

Two of the limitations in §9 are structural rather than fixable: the screen is
bounded by its panel, and it is modality-blind. Both are addressed by a
separate campaign rather than by extending this one.

The [de novo design protocol](denovo_design_protocol.md) reuses this screen's
disease model — the same 112-gene directional signature, the same cached STRING
interactome, the same 74-agent panel — and adds two things this screen lacks:

- a **per-target druggability annotation** (`data/targets/druggability.json`,
  93 targets) that records what each modality can reach, which is what makes
  the modality-blindness above correctable; and
- a **chemistry model** that can represent and measure a structure that does
  not yet exist, which is what lifts the panel bound.

The design campaign independently reproduces this screen's central finding.
Where §6 reports that remyelination-pairing strata outrank every
immunomodulation-only stratum, the design campaign's axis-gap analysis reports
`remyelination = 1.00` and `immunomodulation = 0.00` unmet fraction among
approved agents — the same asymmetry derived by a different route.

It also inherits every input limitation listed above. The signature is still
illustrative; `desired_direction` is still a curation judgement; and the design
layer adds one further assumption of its own — that a pharmacophore keeps its
activity when transplanted onto a scaffold it did not evolve on. Nothing in
that pipeline is docked, synthesised, or assayed, and every designed candidate
carries a machine-readable block naming the thirteen assessment axes on which
nothing has been done.

The generic contracts this screen shares with the design layer — the signature
schema, the effect algebra, the evidence ranking — now live in
`core/biology/signature.py` rather than in `ms_scoring`, which re-exports them.
A second disease (Parkinson's) is registered and exercised end to end, which is
what makes the disease-agnostic claim checkable rather than asserted.

## 11. Relationship to the Parkinson's screen

The [Parkinson's screen](parkinsons_screen.md) is this screen generalised in two
directions, and reading them side by side is the clearest statement of what is
method and what is disease.

- It scores **k = 1, 2, and 3** where this one scores pairs only, so it carries
  a monotherapy baseline and can ask whether a third agent earns its place.
- It reads its pathways, therapeutic axes, risk domains, and risk weights from
  the **registry entry**, where this screen holds them as module constants in
  `ms_scoring`. That is the reason for the second module rather than an edit to
  this one.

Every k-ary term in `core/biology/combination_scoring.py` is defined as the mean
of its pairwise form, so **k = 2 reduces exactly to the definitions used here**
and nothing in this document is being quietly restated in different arithmetic.
Tests pin that reduction for target complementarity, safety union, and Bliss
folding.

This screen has **not** been migrated onto that module; doing so, and re-running
MS at k = 3, is recorded as the next structural task. Until then the two screens
share their contracts and not their scorer, and their absolute scores are not
comparable — different weights, different signature, different panel.

The standing procedure both follow, and the checklist for the third disease, is
in the [disease campaign protocol](disease_campaign_protocol.md).
